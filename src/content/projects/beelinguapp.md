### Research

Learner feedback showed motivation dropped when sessions felt like schoolwork. Stories and illustration were already part of the brand — the opportunity was weaving them deeper into the learning loop.

### Key Insight

When progress felt like finishing a chapter instead of clearing a drill, people came back. Visual storytelling wasn’t decoration; it was the retention mechanic.

### Design & Illustration

I designed flows and illustration systems that kept sessions light, narrative, and habit-friendly — balancing delight with clear learning goals.
