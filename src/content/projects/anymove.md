### Research

Early interviews and ride-alongs revealed that people already pieced together transit, bikes, and rideshares mentally — but existing apps forced them into one mode at a time.

### Key Insight

The product win wasn’t another booking screen; it was helping people compare modes in context (time, cost, effort) before they committed.

### Prototyping

I iterated map-first flows and onboarding prototypes with engineering, tightening the path from “where am I going?” to a confident multimodal choice.
