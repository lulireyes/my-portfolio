## Overview

Helpling connects households with trusted cleaning professionals. I designed and launched a dispute resolution experience that helped operations resolve conflicts faster while keeping the process fair for both sides.

## Role

Product Designer — research, flows, UI, and rollout support with ops and engineering.

## Outcome

- Clearer paths for customers and cleaners to raise and resolve issues
- Reduced operational cost by ~35%
- Improved support efficiency through structured internal tooling
