### Research

A UX audit with brand-side users showed that AI outputs were impressive but hard to place in the existing production pipeline — people didn’t know what to do next.

### Key Insight

Trust came from explainable steps and visible handoffs, not from hiding the model. Redesigning IA around the real idea → production journey unblocked adoption.

### Redesign

I restructured information architecture and UI patterns so AI tools sat inside familiar fashion workflows — with clearer states, previews, and next actions for product teams.
